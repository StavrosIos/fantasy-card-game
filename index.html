Mythos Convergence

A strategic mythology card battle.

How to Play

  • Each player starts with 20 health and 3 cards.
  • Play cards using mana and build your board (max 7 minions).
  • Attack enemy minions or the enemy hero during your turn.
  • Reduce the enemy hero to 0 health to win.
